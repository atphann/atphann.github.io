Machine Failure Prediction: KNN vs Naive Bayes

Course project, BIT-446, Grand Canyon University · Oct 2026

Problem
Predict whether a production machine will fail from hours run, average hours between maintenance and model version.
Method
300 labeled records (of 350) split 70/30 with seed 12345: 210 to train, 90 to test. KNN used the two hour measures after z-score normalization and tested k = 3 to 6. Naive Bayes used all three predictors.
Outcome
KNN scored 96.7% accuracy and caught 12 of 15 failures with no false alarms. Naive Bayes scored 83.3% only by predicting “no failure” for every machine, so it caught none.
Takeaway
Accuracy hid the problem. 75 of 90 machines did not fail, so always predicting “no failure” already scores 83.3%. I would judge a maintenance model on failure recall and missed failures, cross-validate k, test class weighting, and fit normalization on training data only.
